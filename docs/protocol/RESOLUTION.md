# Optimistic resolution and arbitration

This is an optimistic, trusted-arbitration protocol, **not a trustless oracle or UMA integration**. Immutable metadata commits the observation criteria, time zone, sources, YES/NO conditions and ambiguity rules; contracts validate the commitment/URI shape and timing, not real-world truth or IPFS content availability.

P0 integrators should commit `keccak256` of the exact metadata/evidence bytes and verify the retrieved bytes without JSON reserialization; content-address integrity and availability need independent checks. These services are not implemented by the contract.

Anyone except the designated arbitrator may propose YES, NO or INVALID during the explicit proposal window. The creator is eligible to propose but receives no immediate finalization privilege. Nonzero evidence commitments and exact proposal bonds are required. Anyone except proposer/arbitrator may challenge once before the challenge deadline with independent nonzero evidence and the exact challenge bond. Participation in the pool is not required.

An uncontested result can be finalized by anyone only after its challenge window. A challenged result cannot use that route: only the per-market arbitrator can settle it, with a YES/NO/INVALID decision and nonzero decision evidence before the arbitration deadline. Proposal, challenge and decision commitments are stored/emitted; full documents must be independently hosted and verified by future services.

Arbitration uses a contract with multisig owner/threshold views. Governance, compliance and arbitrator owners must be disjoint when validated; threshold >= 2. This check cannot prove deployed code legitimacy, actual signer independence or future owner changes. Operators must verify Safe-compatible code, owner lists, threshold, modules/guards and control separation before release. The arbitrator can lie about a disputed outcome but cannot choose a fund recipient, seize escrow, change a final outcome or extend deadlines. Incorrect real-world outcomes remain a trust/economic risk.

If arbitration fails, anyone cancels at `arbitrationDeadline`. Closed/unfinalized proposals cancel at `maxResolutionTime`. These calls require neither governance nor active/unpaused finance. INVALID/timeout return participant principal without protocol fees. A valid outcome with zero winning participants also enables full refunds. Bond principals return unchanged under all terminal outcomes; see BOND_ECONOMICS.md.

Emergency policy: pause/deactivation blocks payable creation, contributions, proposals and challenges. Every interruption advances a configuration activity epoch. A proposal snapshots its epoch and **cannot finalize uncontested if any pause/deactivation/parameter change occurred afterward**, even after resumption. Such a market can be cancelled at its immutable maximum deadline for full refunds. This conservative rule also invalidates proposals interrupted after the challenge window; it trades availability for preventing challenge censorship followed by uncontested settlement. Existing disputes may still settle through the authorized arbitrator; terminal claims/refunds/bond withdrawals and timeout cancellation remain available. An administrator cannot extend deadlines or select collateral recipients.

## P0.1 amendment

The current [emergency recovery specification](../security/EMERGENCY_RECOVERY.md) replaces the earlier P0 proposal-only wait-until-max rule. Every old-creation-epoch unresolved market is quarantined, and bounded permissionless principal refunds are available without reopening challenge periods. Existing disputes keep their full original arbitration window and require the pinned declared-control fingerprint. Live shape/fingerprint checks cannot prove honest wallet execution or truthful adjudication; deployment verification remains required.
