## Selected technical recovery policy

Safety changes are authorized by the P0.1 task; this is not approval of economics. After any actual interruption, all nonterminal markets created in the old activity epoch are irreversibly quarantined. No contribution/proposal/challenge or uncontested finalization can resume for those markets. All unaffected terminal entitlements remain unchanged. Permissionless emergency cancellation is available after one full snapshotted challenge period from the first interruption, capped by original max deadline; an existing dispute keeps its original arbitration decision/timeout window. This adds an explicit emergency OPEN/CLOSED/PROPOSED → CANCELLED path. It does not reopen markets, change evidence/outcome criteria, redistribute winnings without adjudication, or slash bonds.

This is conservative availability policy: even a short pause aborts unresolved old-epoch markets; privileged emergency actors can force principal refunds rather than an eventual outcome. They already could invalidate proposals in P0. This abort/denial trust must be disclosed and approved before launch. Deadline shortening must never result in a winning settlement. Repeated interruptions cannot postpone the first emergency exit. Configuration updates that deactivate finance also require review and quarantine unresolved markets; economics themselves remain snapshotted.

Release safety uses fresh independent compliance approval plus a fixed 24-hour technical review delay (not a bond/economic parameter), then explicit governance unpause and activation. Approvals commit chain, facade/config identity, parameters, activity epoch and observable role controls. Repeated authorized emergency calls revoke pending approvals but cannot extend old market refund deadlines.

Multisig interfaces do not establish genuine code, signer independence or honest views. Live checks cover declared owners/thresholds/outer runtime hashes; singleton implementations, modules, guards, fallback handlers and hidden shared control still require independently verified deployments and monitoring. Lying implementations are residual blockers, not declared solved.
