## Terminal entitlement and every send

The terminal hook executes in each inherited finalization/ordinary cancellation/emergency cancellation path. It releases exposure exactly once and snapshots NEUTRAL, PROPOSER or CHALLENGERS disposition; it sends no value. Refundable/empty/INVALID/timeout markets are neutral. A valid nonempty binary upheld result allocates P+S to proposer; reversal allocates C_i plus the telescoping P share to each challenger. K returns to its creator. Claims never debit participant or fee liabilities.

`getBondEntitlement` quotes outstanding entitlements from immutable accepted intervals and final S. `claimBond` requires terminal state and the actual recorded owner, marks that owner's claim, validates recipient, debits bond liability and records outflow before the inherited checked send. Failed sends revert every change; zero entitlement is recorded once with no callback. Creation/proposal claims use their original records; CHALLENGE is a per-caller tranche. The old single-owner challenge getter deliberately rejects; use the versioned ABI and `getChallengeStake`. Participant claims/refunds and fee withdrawal continue through their original owner/role-authorized CEI paths. No sweep or admin principal withdrawal was added.

Ratio calculations use ceil divisions on uint128 cap, so 3×cap and2×cap fit uint256. Proposal/creation minima are also bounded by existing ProtocolConfig MAX_BOND. Stake/prefix/S and lifetime liabilities use checked uint256 arithmetic; aggregate S has no policy cap, and overflow reverts atomically. Math.mulDiv supports intermediate products over256bits. A claim amount cannot exceed the deposited K+P+S ledger. Tranche reward differences telescope over contiguous intervals and sum exactly to P; ordering can move atomic rounding units. This is not Sybil resistance. Unclaimed participant dust and bond entitlements stay reserved indefinitely.
