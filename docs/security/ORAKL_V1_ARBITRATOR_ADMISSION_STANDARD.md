# Arbitrator admission standard — proposal pending owner approval

ORAKL's v1 arbiter is trusted to decide truthfully; contract checks do not establish this. The snapshot arbitrator and role fingerprints are enforced by [settleDispute](../../contracts/src/MarketResolver.sol#L87) and [requireArbitrator](../../contracts/src/ProtocolConfig.sol#L173), but observed owner and threshold views cannot prove execution authority or common beneficial control. [P0.3 trust review](P0_3_ARBITRATION_TRUST.md) and its executable residual mocks deliberately demonstrate that boundary. No production wallet is admitted by this document.

## Required evidence before admission

1. Independently obtain deployed runtime bytecode, address, chain, observation block/hash and source/build artifacts; compare hashes, immutable values and deployed identity. Record tool versions, reviewer and reproducible commands; unverified code fails admission.
2. Verify proxy, singleton, implementation and linked libraries including actual implementation storage, all upgrade/change authorities, timelocks and code immutability. Outer proxy codehash alone is insufficient.
3. Enumerate and independently inspect all modules, guards, fallback handlers, delegatecall paths and other hidden or alternative executors; document how each can change, disable or bypass ordinary owner signatures. Deny unknown or unreviewed modules/guards/handlers.
4. Verify actual signers, owner sets, thresholds, key custody, recovery and transaction authorization against reviewed implementation, not merely getters. Require separate human/organizational control and independent investigation of beneficial overlap among governance, compliance, guardian and arbitrator.
5. Establish controlled changes: independent review, notification, monitoring and explicit release reapproval where needed. Revalidate on any owner, threshold, singleton, module, guard, handler or authority change. A restored getter is not evidence of uninterrupted control.
6. Test actual wallet implementation and QIE transaction flow locally and in separately authorized staging; verify signature and guard failure paths, signer availability, gas and ability to submit within the original D/A. No live signing or deployment is authorized here.
7. Adopt an independently auditable evidence workflow: publish accessible rules and current decision queue; continuously index all proposal/challenge/evidence logs including after first challenger and during pauses; verify data and record source authenticity separately from content hashes. A proposed SLA must specify measurable maximum ingestion/review latency comfortably inside the actual immutable window and have independent monitoring; no numerical SLA is silently approved here.
8. Publish conflicts of interest, market ownership/financial-position declarations, recusal and replacement or refund procedure. The current market snapshot does **not** provide arbitrary replacement of its arbitrator; recusal may therefore require the existing expiry/refund path, not an invented on-chain reassignment.
9. Publish reasoned YES/NO/INVALID decision and its evidence commitment; retain source bytes under the evidence policy. Ensure competing evidence, unavailable sources, ambiguity and any failure to meet SLA are recorded.
10. Fund independent monitors, define escalation before D/A, record alerts/acknowledgement, maintain recovery and incident runbooks, including compromised key, abusive emergency pause, failed getters, RPC outage and drift report. On inactivity or invalid authorization, anyone may call the existing deadline-based cancellation; users individually claim principal and bonds.

Freeze requires named accountable owners, wallet reviews and owner acceptance of this trust model, plus external audit. Evidence of workflow adherence cannot mathematically prove truth or signer independence. The owner must explicitly choose any additional appeal or arbiter incentive mechanism before implementation; neither exists in the candidate.

## Selected candidate proposal: dedicated3-of-5 arbitration Safe

The selected local specification calls for a **dedicated3-of-5 Safe design**, not reuse of a governance/compliance wallet. Propose five separately controlled hardware-backed/managed signing authorities, threshold3, with no owner/guardian overlap with governance or compliance and independently reviewed beneficial/control separation. No owner address, wallet deployment, signer provider, native budget or production singleton version is invented or approved here. Simulation uses a mutable five-owner/threshold3 mock, which is not Safe execution evidence.

Before admission, record chain/block/hash/time, exact outer runtime, reviewed Safe singleton and version, storage layout and implementation authority, complete paginated module inventory, guard and fallback handler configuration, owners/threshold, all alternative execution/upgrade authority and any dependencies. Default proposal is no enabled execution modules and no unreviewed guard/fallback; any required component needs its own exact artifact, permission analysis, denial/escape behavior and independent approval. Codehash/getter fingerprints alone cannot establish these facts. Require an independently reproducible read-only admission dossier and controlled local rehearsal using the exact reviewed implementation; genuine signing/wallet/network workflow needs separately authorized operational evidence.

Publish an evidence inbox/SLA, independent reviewer quorum process, source provenance, conflicts/recusal and decision commitment before deployment approval. Review throughout original D, decide only on[D,A), publish reasoning and keep deadline alerts. Unavailable/corrupt signing does not authorize a fallback admin answer: permissionless timeout/refund remains. On key/module/implementation compromise, notify independent guardian/compliance, interrupt new risk, retain evidence and original exits, investigate genuine control changes, and require fresh independent release approval plus24-hour recovery before future activity. Immutable custody/finality has no appeal, upgrade or seizure shortcut. Actual signers, staffing/monitor capital, signed procedures and independent admission remain release blockers.
