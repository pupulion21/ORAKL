# Selected unresolved exposure and bond invariants

The versioned [selected root](../../contracts/src/SelectedMarketFactory.sol) fixes pilotPoolCap from the initial config and immutable global/per-arbitrator bounds3×/2×that tier. These are local candidate ratios; no native Mainnet value or decimals are approved. Each market revalidates the same tier and ratio terms before accepting K. Future config changes do not mutate snapshots or budgets; they still interrupt/quarantine prior epochs.

`totalUnresolvedExposure` and `unresolvedExposureByArbitrator[snapshot]` count actual prediction inflows while nonterminal. Creation, bond deposits and evidence add no exposure. Prediction checks headroom and adds its exact received value atomically with pool/position liability credit. Every terminal path calls a guarded release of YES+NO; claims, refunds, bond claims and fee withdrawals never release it again. Forced native value remains unaccounted surplus. Expiry alone is not a state transition, so keeper cancellation is needed to free stale capacity. Quarantine does not free exposure before the prescribed refund transition.

Unresolved risk is distinct from participant liability: finalized winners and refundable participants still own escrow, fees are a separate liability and bonds another. Repeated terminal creation/settlement with unclaimed balances can grow custody beyond the unresolved cap. Strategic deposits can occupy capacity, and different arbitrator addresses can share beneficial control; the global limit complements address-based limits but cannot bound external hedges or bribery.

The selected [stateful handler](../../contracts/test/invariant/SelectedInvariant.t.sol) keeps independent inflow/outflow, market pools/positions, stake intervals, terminal history, bond payments, fees and interruptions. Eight actors/twelve markets/two simulated arbitrator addresses bound the model; unit tests separately cover128 challengers and huge uint256 stakes. Campaign calls include legitimate early returns, not only complete trades. Four selected assertions check:

1. Exact global conservation and balance = accounted liabilities + forced surplus.
2. Global/per-snapshot-arbitrator exposure equals independent nonterminal contribution sums, budgets hold and release flag matches terminal history.
3. For every terminal market, outstanding quoted entitlements plus prior bond payments equals deposited K+P+S exactly; all markets' unpaid bond principal equals bond liability. Nonterminal quotes are zero. Quotes are compared against a separate integer ghost model.
4. Participant reserves, paid/refunded totals, fees and terminal outcomes match ghost history; no cross-market liability reclassification.

Original A–P assertions and their handler remain unchanged and executable against the P0.3 root; selected handler additionally exercises the changed bond economics, exposure, random claim order, repeated interruptions/recovery, arbitrator switching, getter faults, fees and forced transfers. The old principal-never-slashed assertion is deliberately retained for that old policy, not falsely claimed to describe selected punitive allocation. Original safeguards are inherited by selected and also exercised in selected-specific regressions. No assertion was weakened to accommodate the new policy.
