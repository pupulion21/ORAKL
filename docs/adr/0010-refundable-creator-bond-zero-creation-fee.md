# Refundable Creator Bond K with Zero Nonrefundable Creation Fee F

Market creation deposits are strictly categorized and accounted for as refundable creator bond liabilities (`K`), returned to the creator upon non-abusive market resolution or cancellation. The proposed 0.1% nonrefundable creation fee (`F`) is unapproved and omitted (`F = 0`). The indexer, database, API, and frontend must strictly and consistently distinguish refundable bond liabilities from protocol revenue, ensuring no protocol fee retention occurs at creation or cancellation without explicit authorization.
